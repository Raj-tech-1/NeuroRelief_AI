2. Global shell and navigation

Initial

  • Top Navbar and horizontally scrolling TabBar with 7 tabs: Overview, Cycle, Mood, Symptom, Doctor, Device, Learn.
  • Gradient "Welcome to NeuroRelief AI" banner with one initial button that toggled the profile panel.

Current

  • Sidebar with icons, active highlight, logo, tagline, botanical illustration and italic quote; slide-in drawer on small screens.
  • Header with first-name greeting, tab title and subtitle, language selector, bell, profile menu (Settings, Log out).
  • Floating doctor chat widget on every patient tab.
  • Fit-to-screen mode for Symptom Advisor, Doctor and Device tabs on laptop viewports.

Notification bell

  • Shows doctor responses (accepted/declined) and, with care-sync, a unified feed of appointment events, notes and messages.

Routing

  • ProtectedRoute with role checks and loop-free redirects.
  • New routes /doctor (doctor) and /admin (admin placeholder). Old Navbar/TabBar remain only on /scan, /past-scans and /analyze-report.

8. Patient ↔ Doctor care synchronisation UNCOMMITTED

  • Appointment lifecycle: requested → accepted/declined → patient reschedule or doctor-proposed time → completed/cancelled; each step stored in appointment_events.
  • Messaging: per-appointment encrypted threads, allowed only between the two parties while pending, accepted, or within the follow-up window after completion.
  • Notes and follow-ups: written by the doctor, visible to the patient.
  • Record sharing: patient grants a doctor chosen categories for a date range, purpose and expiry, under pinned consent text records-consent-v1 (SHA-256 verified); revocable. An appointment alone grants no access.
  • Symptom and therapy-session logging endpoints.
  • Shared slot rules (6 slots, weekdays, 60-day horizon, clash detection) for booking and rescheduling.
  • Backend: routes/care.js (~30 endpoints), lib/clinicalService.js, db/clinicalSchema.js (7 tables incl. clinical_audit_events), scripts/migrate-clinical.js, test care-sync.test.mjs.

9. Internationalisation

  • react-i18next with five locale files (en, hi, kn, ta, te; about 1,460 keys each), LanguageSelector, useFormat for numbers, dates and times, localised server messages, an "English only" badge for untranslated content.

  • Dependencies: frontend adds i18next, react-i18next, three, @react-three/fiber, @react-three/drei.
